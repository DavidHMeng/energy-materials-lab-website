Huaimin Jin

Research Assistant Professor

Solid-State Energy Laboratory

He received his Ph.D. from the University of Science and Technology of China in 2024 (advisor: Professor Yang Shangfeng) and his bachelor’s degree from Southwest Jiaotong University in 2019 (advisor: Professor Yang Weiqing) . His primary research focuses on the development of halide solid-state electrolytes and all-solid-state batteries. He has published 12 papers in journals such as *Joule*, *Adv. Mater.*, *Angew.*, and *J. Am. Chem. Soc.* He has led one General Project funded by the China Postdoctoral Science Foundation.
