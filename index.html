Introduction

From Solid-State Materials to Energy Technologies.

The Solid-State Energy Laboratory develops advanced materials and engineering strategies for emerging solid-state energy technologies. Our research bridges fundamental materials chemistry and engineering development, with a current focus on solid-state electrolytes and related energy materials.
Beyond conventional battery systems, we explore new materials, chemistries, and technological directions toward an open and extensible platform for future energy technologies.
